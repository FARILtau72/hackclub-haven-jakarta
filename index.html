If you're in high school or middle school (18 or younger), you are eligible! Beginners, artists, writers, and seasoned coders are all welcome.

100% Free! Meals, snacks, stickers, custom swag, workshops, and prizes are all fully covered thanks to our sponsors.

Don't worry at all! Haven is built for beginners. We run fun workshops and have friendly mentors ready to help you build your very first game from scratch.

Haven events are fully chaperoned by adult supervisors and experienced student leaders. We enforce a strict Code of Conduct and maintain emergency contacts throughout the event.

Join the global Hack Club Slack at hackclub.com/slack and chat with us in the #haven channel, or send us a message anytime!

Yes! Any teenager anywhere in the world can organize a Haven game jam. Hack Club provides grant funding, custom swag, hardware kits, and 1-on-1 mentorship.
